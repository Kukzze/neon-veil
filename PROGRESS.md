- Design decisions / spec fixes:
  - Fire timing carries the remainder while the trigger is held; resetting the cooldown per shot rounds intervals up to whole 64 Hz ticks (950 RPM would fire at 768). Verified: SMG, AR and pistol hold within one shot of their RPM over 10 s; a released trigger never banks extra shots.
  - Recoil settle bound: a critically damped kick at w0 = 22 is at 0.95 % of its peak after 0.35 s, so "within 0.01 degrees in 0.35 s" holds for kicks below ~1.05 degrees (the SMG's 0.6). Tested as written for the SMG and as "< 1 % of peak" for all six weapons; zero overshoot holds for every weapon, including rapid fire.
  - Spread, pellet scatter and recoil yaw use an integer hash seeded by the shot counter instead of `sin`-based noise, so patterns are identical on every platform (needed for networked play).
  - Without a RELOAD bit in the packet, tactical reloads were unreachable; added (protocol v3).
- Simulations (`tests/ranged_sim.rs`, real Avian colliders, 64 Hz): SMG 5-round burst at a dummy 10 m away (ammo 40 -> 35, every round resolved, at least 4 hits, damage and DPS registered, Shock EMP applied, recoil kicked then settled without overshoot); sniper headshot lands on the firing tick for 220 x 3.5 and applies Cryo; a wall stops railgun rounds; emptying the rifle triggers the 2.5 s empty reload and refills from reserve; Thermal Burn deals 8 x 6 extra damage over 4 s then clears; Cryo from a sniper hit slows a sprinting parkour character to 0.55 x 12.8 m/s and wears off after 3.5 s.
